## Proposed argument

AI incident-response products operate near sensitive telemetry and production systems. A comparison is useful only when readers can distinguish vendor claims, primary-source facts, Watchlist testing, practitioner observations, and unresolved questions.

## Evidence required before publication

- Link to the public Watchlist methodology.
- Use source-linked examples that do not imply unperformed testing.
- Ask at least one practitioner to review whether the evidence labels are understandable.
