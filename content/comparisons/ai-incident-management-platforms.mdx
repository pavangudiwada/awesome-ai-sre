## Workflow boundary

The completed page will distinguish detection, triage, investigation, remediation, coordination, communication, and post-incident learning. A feature in one stage must not be presented as coverage of the full lifecycle.

## Required evidence before publication

- Official workflow and product documentation for each capability.
- Clear identification of the product or module being evaluated.
- Human approval, audit trail, access, and rollback details where available.
- Unknown states for pricing, integrations, or controls that are not publicly sourced.
