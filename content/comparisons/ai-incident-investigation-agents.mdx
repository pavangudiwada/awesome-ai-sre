## Evaluation question

Determine how each product investigates a production incident, what access it needs, what it can change, and how an evaluator can verify its work.

## Required evidence before publication

- Primary-source product and security documentation for every material claim.
- A clearly labeled distinction between vendor claims and Watchlist testing.
- Access model, deployment boundary, supported evidence sources, approval controls, and auditability.
- A consistent last-reviewed date based on an actual editorial review.
- No winner, score, or recommendation without a disclosed hands-on test.

## Practitioner validation

- Run the rubric past at least two SRE or platform practitioners.
- Record disagreements and unresolved questions rather than manufacturing consensus.
