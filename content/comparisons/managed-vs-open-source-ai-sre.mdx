## Comparison frame

The completed comparison will separate software licensing from deployment topology. “Open source,” “self-hosted,” and “private deployment” are not interchangeable.

## Required evidence before publication

- License and repository sources for open-source claims.
- Official deployment and data-flow documentation.
- Operator effort, upgrade responsibility, model-provider choices, and support boundaries.
- Unknown fields left visible when a primary source does not answer the question.
